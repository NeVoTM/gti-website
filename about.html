Who we are

GTI delivers both HVM and lab tools spanning compound semiconductors, glass, SiC, quartz, graphene, GaAs, InP, and III-V materials — with engineering that extends tool life and accelerates yield.

Leadership includes veterans of the semiconductor equipment and fab supply chain across Israel, the US, and Asia — bridging chip companies to fabs, OSATs, and production capacity.
